How to animate your logo for Instagram and Reels

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Short answer

The quickest way to animate a logo for Instagram is a template in Canva or CapCut, which takes roughly 1–3 hours for a first version. For a custom animation that matches your brand, you can hire it: Akdeinz Motion’s Logo Sting (3–6 s) is €390 excl. VAT, delivered in 3 working days in 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1, plus a transparent-background render.

Do it yourself

Reels and Stories are vertical (9:16), so build the logo animation vertically first and export a square or landscape version afterwards. You need your logo as a vector file (SVG, AI or PDF) or a large PNG with a transparent background.

Tools

  • Canva: Easiest start. Animation presets on text, shapes and images; exports MP4. Limited control over timing and easing compared with a timeline tool.
  • CapCut: Phone and desktop editor with keyframes, templates and sound. Good for a simple logo reveal. Check the commercial-use terms of any template or music you use.
  • Jitter: Browser-based motion design tool made for short animations such as logo reveals; more control than a template without the learning curve of After Effects.
  • After Effects: Full control and the industry standard. A logo reveal is a good first project, but getting comfortable takes weeks (see the guide on learning After Effects).

Steps

  1. Get the logo as SVG or a high-resolution transparent PNG. A blurry logo cannot be fixed by animation.
  2. Create a 1080 × 1920 px canvas (9:16). Make a 1080 × 1080 px copy for the feed.
  3. Pick one idea: the logo slides in, builds from its shapes, or fades up with one accent movement. Keep it to 3–5 seconds.
  4. Add a short sound: a soft hit or whoosh timed to the moment the logo lands. Use music or sound you have the right to use commercially.
  5. Export as MP4, watch it on your phone with the sound on, then check it on a bright and a dark background.
  6. Export the other ratios. An MP4 cannot carry a transparent background, so a see-through version needs a different export format.

Realistic time for a first logo animation: about 1–3 hours with a Canva or CapCut template, about 3–8 hours in Jitter including learning the tool, and 40+ hours before After Effects feels comfortable.

When DIY is fine

Doing it yourself is a fair choice in these cases:

  • You post casually and a template look is fine.
  • The logo is simple (a wordmark or a single shape) and you only need a short fade or slide.
  • You have no budget this month and plenty of time.
  • You enjoy learning the tool and want to make many small variations yourself.

It is worth hiring when the logo will open every ad and video for months, when it runs in paid ads, or when the hours you would spend are worth more than €390.
