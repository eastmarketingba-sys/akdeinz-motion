How much does motion design cost in 2026?

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Short answer

Motion design usually costs somewhere between a few tens of euros on freelancer marketplaces and several thousand euros at a studio, depending on length, complexity and licence. Akdeinz Motion publishes fixed prices in EUR excl. VAT: from €390 for a 3–6 second Logo Sting (3 working days) to €2,400 for a Brand Film of up to 60 seconds (10–14 working days).

Do it yourself

Doing it yourself looks free, but it costs hours and usually a subscription. The honest comparison is your hours multiplied by what your time is worth, plus tools and licences.

Tools

  • Canva and CapCut: Free tiers exist; paid plans add features and assets. Fast for simple posts.
  • Jitter: Browser-based motion design with more control than a template tool.
  • After Effects: Paid Adobe subscription, plus a long learning curve before the work looks professional.

Steps

  1. Estimate the hours: a first short social animation is often 1–3 hours in a template tool, and a polished 30–60 second video takes far longer.
  2. Multiply the hours by your own hourly rate. Example only: at €50/hour, 10 hours is €500.
  3. Add tool subscriptions and any music, font or stock licences you need.
  4. Compare the total with a fixed quote, and include the risk that the result has to be redone.

A realistic range for a first 30-second promo made from scratch is 6–15 hours. At an example rate of €50/hour that is €300–€750 of your time, before any subscription.

What drives the price of motion design

  • Length: every extra second needs more design and animation work.
  • Complexity: custom illustration and character work cost more than animated text and shapes.
  • Formats: 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1 each need their own layout (Akdeinz Motion includes all three).
  • Revisions: how many rounds of changes are included and what an extra one costs.
  • Music, sound design and voiceover: licensed audio has to be paid for by someone.
  • Licence: where and for how long you may use the video, including paid ads.
  • Speed: rush work is normally priced higher (Akdeinz Motion: +35% under 72 hours).

When DIY is fine

A cheaper route is fine when:

  • The video is short-lived, for example a one-week promotion.
  • You need many quick variations and a template look is acceptable.
  • You are validating an idea before spending real budget.
  • You have a monthly volume low enough that the hours are not a problem.

If you need several ads each month, a retainer is cheaper per ad: Motion Monthly 4 is 4 × Social Motion Ad S for €1,590 per month (€397.50 per ad instead of €490), and Motion Monthly 8 is €2,890 per month (€361.25 per ad), with a 3-month minimum.
