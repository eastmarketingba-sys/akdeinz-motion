Canva or CapCut vs a professional motion designer

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Short answer

Canva and CapCut are good enough for quick, template-based social videos. A professional motion designer is worth it when the video represents your brand for months, runs as a paid ad, or needs movement a template cannot give you. Akdeinz Motion starts at €390 for a Logo Sting and €490 for a Social Motion Ad of up to 15 seconds (3–4 working days), excl. VAT.

Do it yourself

Both tools are built for speed. You pick a template, swap in your text, logo and footage, and export. The trade-off is that the result looks like the template.

Tools

  • Canva: Strong on layout, brand kit and team sharing. Animation is applied per element with presets; fine control over timing and easing is limited compared with a timeline editor.
  • CapCut: Real timeline with keyframes, captions, effects and templates, on phone and desktop. Check the commercial-use terms of templates, effects and music before using them in ads.
  • Jitter and After Effects: The next step up when templates stop being enough. Jitter is lighter to learn; After Effects offers full control at the price of a long learning curve.

Steps

  1. Choose a template that fits the format you need (9:16 for Reels, 1:1 or 16:9 for other placements).
  2. Replace the placeholder text, logo and footage with your own, using your brand colours and fonts.
  3. Shorten it. Social ads that get to the point in the first seconds usually hold attention better than slow intros.
  4. Replace any template music that you are not licensed to use commercially.
  5. Export and check the file on a phone, with and without sound.

A realistic time for a 15-second social video from a template: about 1–3 hours for the first, less once you reuse your own template.

Where templates fall short

  • Sameness: many accounts use the same templates, so the video can look like everyone else’s.
  • Control: custom timing, easing and transitions are limited in template tools.
  • Sound: sound design (not just a music track) needs time and a trained ear.
  • Formats: adapting one video to 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1 by hand is repetitive work.
  • Licensing: template and music terms are easy to overlook, and paid ads raise the stakes.
  • Hours: the time you spend is time you are not spending on running the business.

When DIY is fine

Canva or CapCut is the right call when:

  • The video is for organic posts on your own channels and has a short life.
  • You need a quick variation of something you already have.
  • Speed matters more than a distinctive look.
  • You enjoy making them and the volume is low.

Hire when the video will run as a paid ad, represents the brand for months, or you need several every month. For that, Motion Monthly 4 is €1,590 per month for 4 × Social Motion Ad S (3-month minimum).

Frequently asked questions

Is Canva good enough for business videos?

For quick organic posts, often yes. For paid ads or videos that carry your brand for months, a custom piece usually looks more distinctive. Check licence terms of every template and track either way.

Is CapCut allowed for commercial use?

It depends on the specific template, effect or music track and on your account type. Check CapCut’s current terms before using them in ads.
