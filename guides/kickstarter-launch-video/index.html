How to make a Kickstarter launch video

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Short answer

A Kickstarter launch video has to show what the product is, who is behind it and why someone should back it, in the first seconds. Most founders can put together a basic one themselves in roughly 15–40 hours. If you want an animated one, Akdeinz Motion’s Explainer (up to 60 s) is €1,900 excl. VAT and a Product Promo (30–45 s) is €1,290, delivered in 10–14 and 7 working days.

Do it yourself

Plan the video before you open any tool. The campaign page is where backers decide, so the video should answer three questions quickly: what is it, why does it matter, and what do I get if I back it.

Tools

  • CapCut: Timeline editor for combining phone footage, captions and music. Check commercial-use terms of templates and music.
  • Canva: Simple editor for text-led or screenshot-led videos.
  • Jitter: Browser-based tool for animating product graphics or UI.
  • After Effects: Best for custom animated product explanations, but needs weeks of learning (see the guide on learning After Effects).

Steps

  1. Write a one-page script: the problem, your product as the solution, how it works, who you are, what backers get and the call to action.
  2. Collect real footage: the prototype or product in use, the team or founder, and any workshop or testing shots. Real prototypes build more trust than renders.
  3. Record clear audio. A voiceover or the founder speaking to the camera matters more than fancy visuals.
  4. Edit to the script. Put the product and its main benefit in the opening seconds, and cut anything that does not help someone decide.
  5. Add captions, since many people watch without sound, and music you have the right to use commercially.
  6. Check Kickstarter’s current guidance for campaign videos before you finalise, and export a 16:9 file for the page.

A realistic range for a first launch video is about 15–40 hours across scripting, gathering footage, editing and revisions. Animated explainer sections add more.

What to put in a launch video

  • The hook: the problem or the most striking shot of the product.
  • The product: what it is and how it works, shown rather than described.
  • The people: who is making it and why they are qualified to deliver it.
  • The offer: what backers receive and when, kept consistent with the campaign page.
  • The ask: one clear call to action at the end.

Reuse the video beyond the campaign page

A launch video is also material for social teasers and paid ads. Akdeinz Motion delivers 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1 versions of every package, so the same piece can run on the campaign page, in Reels and in feed ads.

When DIY is fine

Making it yourself is a reasonable choice when:

  • Your story depends on you speaking on camera and showing the real product.
  • The budget is the campaign itself, and every euro matters before launch.
  • You have enough weeks before launch to make, review and revise it.
  • You have good phone footage and only need to cut it together with text and captions.

Consider hiring if the product is hard to explain with footage alone, you want animated sequences, or you cannot afford 15–40 hours before launch. Plan backwards from your launch date and leave time for your own feedback on the preview.
