How long does it take to learn After Effects well enough for a brand video?

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Short answer

Starting from zero, expect roughly 55–110 hours of practice before After Effects work looks good enough for a brand video, which is about 11–22 weeks at 5 hours a week. If your time is worth more than the price, hiring is cheaper: a Logo Sting is €390 in 3 working days and a Brand Film is €2,400 in 10–14 working days, excl. VAT.

Do it yourself

After Effects is the standard tool for motion design, and it can do almost anything. The cost is the learning curve. The hours below are estimates for a person with no animation background who practises on real projects.

Tools

  • After Effects: Industry standard timeline and compositing tool. Paid Adobe subscription.
  • Jitter: A lighter, browser-based route to simple brand animations while you decide whether to commit to After Effects.
  • CapCut and Canva: Faster for simple posts, but they do not replace After Effects skills for custom brand work.

Steps

  1. Basics (about 10–20 hours): interface, compositions, layers, keyframes, easing and the graph editor.
  2. Shapes, text and masks (about 15–30 hours): building logo reveals, lower thirds and animated text from scratch.
  3. Structure and speed (about 20–40 hours): pre-compositions, parenting, simple expressions and reusable templates.
  4. Sound, export and polish (about 10–20 hours): syncing to audio, motion principles, exporting for different ratios and platforms.

Adding the stages gives roughly 55–110 hours. At 5 hours a week that is 11–22 weeks. Each extra aspect ratio and each revision then takes more of your time.

What your hours are worth

Multiply the hours by your own hourly rate. As an example only, at €50/hour, 55–110 hours is €2,750–€5,500 of your time, before the software subscription.

For comparison, a Brand Film of up to 60 seconds with Akdeinz Motion is €2,400 excl. VAT, delivered in 10–14 working days, and a Logo Sting is €390 in 3 working days.

When DIY is fine

Learning After Effects yourself makes sense when:

  • You want motion design as a skill or a career, not only one video.
  • You will make a steady stream of videos for years, so the learning cost is spread out.
  • You enjoy the craft and have the weeks to spend.
  • Your needs are small and a lighter tool such as Jitter would do.

Frequently asked questions

Can I learn After Effects in a month?

You can learn the basics in a few weeks, but reaching brand-video quality usually takes longer. Our estimate is 55–110 hours of practice.

Is After Effects worth learning for a small business?

Only if you will make videos regularly or want the skill. For one or a few videos, the hours usually cost more than hiring.

What is an easier tool than After Effects?

Jitter, Canva and CapCut are easier to learn but give less control.
