What we make

  • Logo stings: 3–6 seconds, from €390.
  • Social motion ads: up to 15 s from €490, up to 30 s from €790.
  • Product promos: 30–45 seconds, €1,290.
  • Explainers: up to 60 seconds, €1,900.
  • Brand films: up to 60 seconds, €2,400.
  • Monthly retainers: 4 or 8 social ads a month, 3-month minimum.

Who it is for

Founders and small brands who are making motion content themselves, or thinking about it. We work with clients in Belgium and the EU first, the United States second, and anywhere else.

The pitch is time. Learning After Effects well enough for a brand video is an estimated 55–110 hours [VERIFY]. A Logo Sting is €390 and 3 working days. The guides show the numbers for each tool.

How it works

  1. Get a quote: tell us what you need, the length and the deadline.
  2. Pay 50% to start.
  3. Review a watermarked preview and send up to 2 rounds of revisions.
  4. Approve the preview and pay the remaining 50%.
  5. Receive the rendered video in 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1, 1080p MP4.

What you get, and what you do not

You get the rendered video files, licensed music and sound design, and a worldwide, perpetual commercial licence for your own channels and paid ads.

You do not get source or project files. The deliverable is the video.

The mark

The mark joins an A and an M into one continuous form, built from parallel bands cut at 60°. Where the bands overlap, a soft fold catches the light, like film or ribbon pulled through a frame.
