Motion design pricing

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Short answer

Akdeinz Motion motion design costs from €390 (Logo Sting, 3–6 s, 3 working days) to €2,400 (Brand Film, up to 60 s, 10–14 working days). Prices are in EUR, excl. VAT. Every package includes 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1 versions in 1080p MP4, licensed music and sound design, 2 revision rounds and a commercial licence.

Frequently asked questions

How much does motion design cost at Akdeinz Motion?

Prices start at €390 for a Logo Sting (3–6 s) and go up to €2,400 for a Brand Film (up to 60 s). Social Motion Ad S (up to 15 s) is €490, Ad M (up to 30 s) is €790, Product Promo (30–45 s) is €1,290 and Explainer (up to 60 s) is €1,900. All prices are in EUR, excl. VAT.

What is included in every package?

16:9, 9:16 and 1:1 versions, 1080p MP4, licensed music and sound design, 2 revision rounds, and a worldwide, perpetual commercial licence for your own channels and paid ads. Logo Sting also includes a transparent-background render.

How does payment work?

50% to start, 50% after you approve a watermarked preview.

Do I get the project files?

No. The deliverable is the rendered video file only. Project and source files are not offered with any package.

How much does extra length cost?

For Product Promo, Explainer and Brand Film, each extra 15 seconds is +€600 and +2 working days.

Can I get a monthly plan?

Yes. Motion Monthly 4 is €1,590 per month for 4 × Social Motion Ad S and Motion Monthly 8 is €2,890 per month for 8 × Social Motion Ad S. Both have a 3 months minimum.

What does rush delivery cost?

Delivery in under 72 hours is +35%. 4K delivery is +20%.
