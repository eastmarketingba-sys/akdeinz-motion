What I did

A 9-second data animation. A bar chart by month has a blue average line, and three key figures sit under it: 63%, a trend line and 11,871 units.

All numbers are sample data made for this piece. It is a self-initiated piece made for the Akdeinz Motion studio. No client is shown.

Why I did it

A table of numbers is rarely read. This piece shows how a chart can be set up so the key figure stands out.
